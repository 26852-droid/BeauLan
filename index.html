Welcome to Scotland

Scotland is a country in the United Kingdom. It is famous for its beautiful mountains, castles, lochs and culture.

Scotland has many interesting places to visit. This website shows some famous places and traditions.

Famous Places in Scotland

Here are some famous places in Scotland.

Edinburgh Castle

Edinburgh Castle is a famous castle in Edinburgh. It is built on Castle Rock and has a long history.

It is one of the most popular tourist attractions in Scotland.

The Isle of Skye

The Isle of Skye is a beautiful island in Scotland. It is famous for its mountains and coastline.

Many people visit Skye to see its natural scenery.

Loch Ness

Loch Ness is a large lake in the Scottish Highlands. It is famous for the legend of the Loch Ness Monster.

The area around Loch Ness has beautiful scenery.

Scottish Culture

Scotland has many famous traditions.

Bagpipes

Bagpipes are a traditional Scottish instrument.

Kilt

The kilt is a traditional Scottish piece of clothing.

Haggis

Haggis is a traditional Scottish food.
